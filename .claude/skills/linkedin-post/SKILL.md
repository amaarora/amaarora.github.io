## LinkedIn Post Writing

Help Aman craft LinkedIn posts, primarily for promoting blog posts. This is a collaborative process - draft, iterate, and refine together.

### Voice & Tone

- **First person, direct** - same voice as the blog
- **Confident but not arrogant** - share what you know without talking down to anyone
- **Never derogatory** - avoid anything that implies the reader or others are deficient. "Everyone can build a demo" was flagged as derogatory. Frame challenges as shared industry problems, not individual shortcomings.
- **No employer references** - do not reference previous workplaces or "systems I have shipped" in ways that tie to a specific company
- **No dramatic/theatrical statements** - keep it grounded

### Structure That Works

Based on the RoPE post that got 1,074 likes:

1. **Hook (first 1-2 lines)** - must stop the scroll. This is what shows before "see more". Use a specific, relevant claim or question. The RoPE post opened with a specific technical fact that readers recognized.
2. **Build curiosity** - create a gap between what the reader knows and what you're about to share
3. **The value** - what you're sharing (the numbered list, the framework, the resource)
4. **Tease the depth** - mention what's inside (infographics, examples, walkthroughs) so people know it's worth clicking
5. **Close** - keep it short. "Link in comments!" works.

### Formatting

- Short paragraphs - 1-2 sentences max per block
- Numbered lists for frameworks/stages - include one-line descriptions
- White space between every block (LinkedIn rewards readability)
- No hashtags unless Aman specifically asks
- "Link in comments!" at the end (LinkedIn deprioritizes posts with links in the body)

### What to Avoid

- Generic LinkedIn motivational language ("The future is now", "Let that sink in")
- Overselling ("This will change how you think about...")
- Referencing specific employers or clients
- Softening the hook too much - toned-down versions lose engagement
- Long closing paragraphs - the list does the selling

### Process

1. Read the blog post to understand the key points
2. Draft the post collaboratively with Aman
3. Aman provides brain dumps via voice - clean up while preserving intent
4. Iterate on hook, body, and close separately
5. Check every line for: derogatory tone, employer references, accuracy of claims
